## P3 deviations (all made before the first complete synthetic Monte Carlo)

6. **Pitch-hold gains lowered from (1.0, 0.3) to (0.3, 0.05)** deg per deg and deg per deg/s. The first gains put a 3.8 to 9.6 Hz closed-loop pitch pole in the loop (open-loop short period 1.2 to 1.8 Hz); 54% of the Cm signal power sat above 5 Hz, so Cm from 50 Hz logs read 0.48 of truth on noise-free data (`scripts/diag/sysid_clean.py`). New gains keep the closed-loop poles at or below 2.3 Hz. The aborted first run `sysid-synth20-20261118-112504-e320cdc` is kept and marked aborted; it is not used.
7. **Glide windows = scheduled steady-glide segments.** The threshold detector (|dV/dt| < 0.15 m/s^2, |q| < 0.03 rad/s for 4 s) found fewer than 3 windows per flight under light turbulence, so CD0 and k were undefined. The force-method windows are now each 15 s steady-glide segment (attitude hold, no excitation) split into three 5 s windows, 18 windows per flight. Definition only; the V15 bar is unchanged.
8. **Equation-error Cm uses consistent 5 Hz zero-phase filtering and a 4th-order derivative** (Klein and Morelli 2006). A known residual bias remains on noise-free data: Cma -4.3%, Cmq -5.7%, Cmde -4.5% (CL derivatives within 0.3%). Root cause traced, not fixed: the controller updates the elevator every 5 ms (200 Hz) while the log samples it every 20 ms, so the logged elevator is a sample of a staircase that the airframe integrates; the integrated q and the sampled de disagree by a fraction of a sample (`scripts/diag/qdot_true.py`: d/dt of logged q is 0.92 of the simulator's own q_dot at log instants even after 3 Hz filtering). Ratios such as trim elevator are unaffected (all Cm terms scale together); static margin -Cma/CLa inherits the 4% bias. Reported, not corrected.
9. **Errors-in-variables correction added to equation error (post hoc, after the second Monte Carlo).** Run `sysid-synth20-20261118-115056-030dcbe` (superseded, kept) gave median CLa error 44%, Cma 19%, Cmde 18%, Cmq 20%: the 0.5 deg vane noise is as large as the part of alpha that is independent of elevator and pitch rate (residual std 0.010 rad, predicted attenuation 0.57, observed 0.56; `scripts/diag/sysid_noise.py`). Fix: CL is filtered with the same 5 Hz filter as the regressors, and the alpha regressor gets bias-compensated least squares with the vane noise variance estimated from the data (robust second-difference estimator, no use of the simulator's noise setting). CD0 and k (glide force method) were already within 2% and are unchanged.
10. **V16 CI definition unchanged; a calibration-inclusive CI is reported as secondary.** The same superseded run showed 25% CD0 coverage: the window-regression SE ignores the per-flight pitot scale error (N(1, 0.01)), which biases every coefficient by 2x the scale error. V16 is scored with the pre-registered SEs. `coverage_with_calibration_pct` adds the 2 x 1% calibration term in quadrature and is labelled post hoc.
11. **Euler stopping rule.** The first 7 compressible runs stopped on rms density < -11. On the medium/fine wedge grids the limiter held the residual in a limit cycle near -4.7 for 6000 iterations while CL and CD were stationary to 1e-9. Remaining runs stop on a Cauchy criterion on CD (1e-7 over 500 iterations); every run reports both residual drop and force stationarity.

12. **Leading-edge orifice comparison fixed (post hoc, analysis only, after the first cut-line analysis).** The TM-4062 orifice at x/c = 0 was compared with an upper-surface interpolation clamped to the first upper wall node, which read Cp -0.11 against a measured +0.58 at alpha 4 and alone contributed most of the RMS. It is now compared with the CFD wall node of minimum x (the leading edge itself). Effect on RMS dCp upper at Re 200k: SA a0 0.053 to 0.051, a4 0.142 to 0.096, a8 0.283 to 0.212; SST-LM a0 0.044 to 0.041, a4 0.131 to 0.078, a8 0.312 to 0.247. No solver input changed. The V06 bar (0.05) is unchanged and still fails.
13. **Euler convergence classifier fixed and one wedge run redone (post hoc, after the first compressible post-processing).** The first classifier took the max relative change of CL and CD over the last 500 iterations; most diamond runs stop at 450 to 590 iterations, so that window included the start-up transient, and CL is 0 at alpha 0, which made 8 converged runs read "not-converged". The classifier now uses: rms[Rho] drop >= 6 orders, or SU2's own CD Cauchy stop (read from log.txt, "Cauchy[CD] ... Yes") before the 6000 cap AND CD max relative deviation over the last 100 iterations < 1e-6 from history.csv. Every diamond run meets one of these; their CD deviation over the last 100 iterations is at most 5e-8. The wedge M2 fine run ran under the first stopping rule (rms < -11, 100-element Cauchy 1e-10), hit the cap in a residual limit cycle (3.1 orders, CD deviation 3.5e-6), so it is rerun under the Cauchy rule as `comp-wedge-M2-a0-L2-20261118-181717-0402430`; the wedge M2 coarse run (same first rule, cap, 4.2 orders, CD deviation 4.5e-8) is rerun the same way as `comp-wedge-M2-a0-L0-20261118-182256-0402430`. Old runs are kept and marked superseded. After the reruns all 24 runs are converged (14 residual, 10 Cauchy). Provenance bug found on the way: `su2_euler.run` rewrote case.cfg of already finished runs with the current template; it now returns before writing, and log.txt is the record of what actually ran for the first 7 runs.
14. **Loop engine restricted to the identification envelope (post hoc, after the first blind run).** Run `loop-blind6-20261118-121817-184e9a1` (superseded, kept) failed V18 with CD0 delta error up to 24.8 pp, SM delta error up to 8.0% MAC, sink error cut 4.6%, trim error cut 20.0%. Root cause: the injected aft CG (2 to 5% MAC) lowers the static margin from 0.115 to about 0.07 to 0.09, so the same elevator excitation drove 10 to 12% of flight-1 samples beyond 9 deg alpha, up to 16 to 17 deg (stall at 11.4 deg), where the model is not linear; equation error then gave Cmq +0.08 (truth -8.9) and CLa +32%. On the same log, excluding samples with vane alpha > 9 deg (the linear identification envelope stated in `flightloop/sim/truth.py`; PREREG 12 states the flown envelope as alpha <= 9.6 deg, and the engine uses the stricter 9 deg) gives CLa -1.9%, Cma -3.4%, Cmde -5.7%, CD0 -1.1%. The engine now applies this mask to equation error and drops glide windows whose alpha exceeds 9 deg; the threshold comes from the design model, not from the sealed truth. The scoring metric for V18 is unchanged (inertial sink = mean vd over each flight-2 glide segment; trim = mean elevator). A secondary, post hoc metric is reported next to it: air-relative sink V sin(alpha - theta) from measured TAS, vane alpha and pitch, because a 15 s mean of inertial vd carries the air-mass vertical velocity (Dryden length scale about 35 s at 15 m/s), which no aerodynamic model can predict; and the same restricted to flight-2 glides inside the envelope. The synthetic V15/V16 pipeline (`run_sysid_synth.py`, 1.2% of samples beyond 9 deg) is unchanged.
